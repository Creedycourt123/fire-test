CCSH FIRE ALARM TEST: STANDALONE APP (TRIAL)
=============================================

WHAT IS IN THIS FOLDER
  index.html        The app itself
  manifest.json     Tells the phone the app's name, colours and icon
  sw.js             Lets the app work with no signal
  pdf-lib.min.js    Makes the PDFs
  4 .png files      The app icon in different sizes

Keep all of these files together in one folder. Do not rename them.
There are no subfolders, so every file can be uploaded in one go,
even from a phone.

This version does not need Claude. Everything (finished weeks, your
name, signature and door names) is saved on the phone only. It works
with no signal once it has been opened once.


PART 1: PUT THE APP ONLINE (FREE, ABOUT 20 MINUTES)
---------------------------------------------------
The app needs a web address before it can be installed. GitHub Pages
gives you one for free.

1. Go to github.com and create a free account.
2. Tap the + at the top right and choose "New repository".
3. Name it fire-test, tick "Public", then tap "Create repository".
4. Tap "uploading an existing file".
5. Select every file from this folder (all 9 of them, the guide is
   optional) and tap "Commit changes".
   Check the files sit at the top of the repository, NOT inside a
   folder. If you see a folder called "CCSH Fire Test App..." the app
   will not install; delete it and upload the files themselves.
6. Go to Settings, then Pages (on the left).
7. Under "Branch" choose "main" and "/ (root)", then tap Save.
8. After a minute or two the page shows your web address, for example:
   https://yourname.github.io/fire-test/
   Open it on your phone to check the app loads.

The app holds no care records, only device names, so it is safe for
this page to be public. Your test results never leave the phone.


PART 2, OPTION A: INSTALL IT ON YOUR PHONE (2 MINUTES)
------------------------------------------------------
1. Open your web address in Chrome on your Android phone.
2. Tap the three dots menu, then "Install app" (or "Add to Home screen").
3. It now has its own icon, opens full screen like any app, and
   works with no signal.

For most people this is all you need.

IF IT SAYS "THIS APP CANNOT BE INSTALLED"
• Check the address ends in github.io, not github.com. The github.com
  page only shows the files; it is not the app.
• Check the files are at the top of the repository and not in a folder
  (see Part 1, step 5).
• If you uploaded an older copy that had an "icons" folder, delete
  everything in the repository and upload this new copy instead.
• Wait two minutes after uploading, then close the tab, reopen the
  address and pull down to refresh before trying again.
• Work phones managed by the company often block installing apps.
  If so, tap the three dots menu, then "Add to Home screen", then
  choose "Create shortcut". It still gets an icon on the home screen,
  works with no signal and keeps your finished weeks. The only
  difference is that it opens with Chrome's address bar showing.


PART 2, OPTION B: MAKE A REAL ANDROID APP FILE (ABOUT 30 MINUTES)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to pwabuilder.com.
2. Paste in your web address and tap "Start".
3. Tap "Package for stores", then "Android", then "Generate Package".
4. Download the zip it makes. Inside is an .apk file (to install on
   phones directly) and an .aab file (for the Google Play Store).
5. To install the .apk: copy it to the phone, open it, and allow
   "Install unknown apps" when asked.
6. To put it on the Google Play Store you need a Google Play developer
   account (a one off fee of 25 US dollars) and to upload the .aab file.
   This is only worth doing if lots of staff will use it.


UPDATING THE APP LATER
----------------------
1. Upload the new index.html to your GitHub repository (replace the old one).
2. Open sw.js on GitHub, tap the pencil, and change
   fire-test-v1 to fire-test-v2 (then v3 next time, and so on).
   This tells phones to fetch the new version.
3. Phones pick up the update the next time the app is opened with signal.


THINGS TO KNOW
--------------
• If you uninstall the app or clear Chrome's data, the record of
  finished weeks is lost. The PDFs you saved or emailed are not affected.
• Each phone keeps its own record, so use the same phone each time.
• To share the PDF, use "Email or share PDF" after creating it, as before.
